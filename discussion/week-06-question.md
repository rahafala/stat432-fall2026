The lecture's KNN experiment has one predictor, so Manhattan and Euclidean distance pick the same neighbors. With two or more predictors, though, they can pick different neighbors. In that case, would using Manhattan distance increase or decrease the variance of the fitted probability $\hat{p}(x)$ for a fixed $k$? Would it change the bias–variance tradeoff for better or worse?
