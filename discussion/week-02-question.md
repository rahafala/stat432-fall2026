title: "Model fit vs complexity"
author: "Rahaf Alabdullah (rahafaa2)"
---

As model complexity increases, training error usually decreases because the model can fit the observed data more closely. However, testing error may eventually increase when the model becomes too flexible and overfits. How do Cp, AIC, and BIC balance this tradeoff between improving fit and penalizing complexity? In what sense do these criteria aim to estimate the model that generalizes best, rather than the one that minimizes training error? Why might BIC often prefer a simpler model than AIC or Cp, especially when sample size is large?
