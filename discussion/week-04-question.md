title: "Choosing Ridge vs. Lasso in Big Models"
author: "Rahaf Alabdullah (rahafaa2)"
---

Ridge and Lasso both penalize model complexity, but choosing between them changes a lot as the number of predictors grows. When predictors are highly correlated, is Ridge safer? When we expect only a few predictors matter, does Lasso's sparsity win, despite instability under collinearity?
