title: "K-Means or Hierarchical for Tissue Groups?"
author: "Rahaf Alabdullah (rahafaa2)"
---

What if a lab has gene expression data for about 190 samples but no tissue labels, and does not know how many groups exist? Some groups may be nested (for example, two brain regions), and some may be much smaller than others. Would k-means or hierarchical clustering be better here? In particular, how does each method handle choosing the number of clusters, nested structure, and very small groups? Without labels, how could we tell which result to trust?
