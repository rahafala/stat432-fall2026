Question 6

The 39 million daily records are not independent observations because repeated observations from the same person are correlated. Participants differ in how many days they contribute, and averaging all days thus weights people unequally.
BYOD and WEAR groups differ in socioeconomic and access factors, so the comparison does not establish causation.
A very large dataset can still be biased if the sample is selected or if only some people contribute many days, so the naive average of all recorded days may not represent the true US adult target.
